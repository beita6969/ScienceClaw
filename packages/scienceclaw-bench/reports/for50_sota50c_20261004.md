# FoR50 SOTA50C disjoint formal batch (2026-10-04)

This batch used the existing visible-train-only `fit_predict` route on new deterministic episodes `skip=12`; it did not repeat the `00–11` items used by SOTA50, SOTA50B, or H50C. The manifest was added as `SOTA50C` with ID/OOD four episodes each and required tool `fit_predict`.

## Results

| split | item suffixes | primary F1 | z=1 | hard/completed/reproducible |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ID | 12, 13, 14, 15 | 0.419043, 0.558423, 0.458324, 0.345460 (mean 0.445312) | 3/4 | 4/4 |
| OOD | 12, 13, 14, 15 | 0.132664, 0.254605, 0.272161, 0.298330 (mean 0.239440) | 0/4 | 4/4 |

The one ID failure and all four OOD failures are ordinary acceptance failures (`z=0`); the result receipts are complete, reproducible, within budget, and hard-valid. The split-scoped validator therefore reports the expected `z must be integer 1` errors and no infrastructure or tool-lineage error. The trajectories are retained under `$L/sc-runs/toolon_SOTA50C_{id,ood}`.

## Interpretation

This disjoint batch reinforces that FoR50 OOD is materially harder than the visible-train diagnostic and that the low scores are not caused by a scorer crash, split omission, or malformed output. It is a new formal evidence batch, not a reason to rewrite the acceptance line or combine failed items with earlier batches. The public DeBERTa checkpoint remains excluded because its stated 9,324 labeled examples cover the project data boundary.
