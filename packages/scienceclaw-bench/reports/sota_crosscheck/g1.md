Live leaderboard page (phenobench.org) returned 404, so challenge overview numbers come from the secondary table above.

**Comparability**: only roughly comparable. Same PQ+ definition, but ours is scored at 512x512 (nearest `::2` subsample, `Adapter(scale=2)`, lines 374-381, 464-476), 16 images/episode, CPU classical pipeline, 24 labelled source + 8 dev images, versus hundreds/thousands of training images and GPU nets. Slice noise: SD of the episode mean 5.4 PQ+ (SE of 5-ep mean about 2.4); OOD (06-05 growth stage, overlapping plants) is systematically harder for IoU_weed (about 2). Gap to any published method (>= 20 points) is far outside noise.

**Parity** (`g1_work/parity30.py`, official `phenobench.evaluation.*` vs `payload_metrics`/`hierarchical_image_stats`, real GT with seeded perturbations): at 1024 all 7 terms agree to <= 0.01 (PQ+ 57.0525 vs 57.0541; 62.385 vs 62.3835). At scale 2 PQ+ drifts +0.4 to +1.0 and PQ_crop +1.3 to +3.5 (nearest downsampling removes small leaf/plant boundaries), i.e. our score is slightly optimistic vs full-res. PQ+ = mean(IoU_soil, IoU_weed, PQ_crop, PQ_leaf) matches (line 236); visibility <= 0.5 partial-mask filter matches (153-174).

**Leakage / easy path**: no label path to eval images found (eval arrays from the adapter only, source labels exposed by design, `expose_source_labels`, line 374); id/val/ood images are pool-disjoint and OOD is a later capture. Trivial outputs (`floor30.py`): all-soil = 24.2 (train) / 24.4 (val); perfect semantics with no instances = 50.0 by construction. The ExG reference (19.9 val e00, 24.6/26.5 id, 33.2/30.1 ood) sits at or below the all-soil floor on 2 of 5 episodes (val e00 19.9, id e00 24.6; floor measured on the 8-image smoke subsets, so approximate), so "z=1" is a weak signal there.

**Verdict: BELOW-PEERS.** Soil IoU is at peer level (95-99.6 vs 98.5-99.4). The gap is weed IoU (2-35 vs 61-74), PQ_crop (22-36 vs 55-82) and PQ_leaf (7-12 vs 47-75). Missing component: a pretrained instance-segmentation backbone (Mask2Former/SAM class) that separates touching crop plants and leaves; label-efficient CPU pipeline cannot. Not a scoring bug.

**To-do**
1. `for30_phenobench.py:374-381,464-476`: document (or default to scale 1 for the reported paper number) that scale 2 inflates PQ_crop by up to 3.5 points; never quote 512-px numbers next to leaderboard values without the caveat.
2. `for30_phenobench.py:259-278` (`exg_reference`) and `:79` (`ACCEPT_MARGIN`): reference is below the trivial all-soil floor (24.4) on val e00 (19.9); use `max(ExG, all-soil)` as reference or add the floor to eval.json so acceptance is not "beat a weak baseline".
3. Report the 4 components separately per episode (already in eval.json) and never present the pooled PQ+ as comparable to the challenge.

| Table 3: Persistence Ensemble (= 7-day hour mean) | 16.68 | 77.88 | 47.3 | 0.82 |

The "balanced" column is my arithmetic (paper reports the two medians only).

**Comparability**: NOT comparable in absolute value, ratios only. Paper: about 1900 real buildings, year-long day-ahead evaluation, one CVRMSE per building over the year, median per category. Ours: 3-11 buildings per role, 24-48 evaluation hours per building (`M_TARGET`, line 80; id has 1 window/building with 16 items), so one building's CVRMSE is a 1-2 day statistic and the median of 3-5 buildings is very noisy: per-episode agent/prev-day ratio ranges 0.64-1.08. Our agent also sees 3601 h of the target building (`load_history`, line 541), i.e. the paper's transfer/fine-tune regime (Table 4), not zero-shot.

**Parity** (`parity33.py`, `rescore33.py`): CVRMSE (`nrmse_pct`, line 276) equals `buildings_bench.evaluation.metrics` (max diff 1.4e-14); category median (290) and balanced mean (296) follow `aggregate.py`. Same-slice rescoring with the official baselines: agent/avg7 = 0.93, 1.09, 0.89, 0.87, 0.89 (mean 0.93; paper best model vs avg7 = 45.1/47.7 = 0.95), agent/prev-day mean 0.79 (paper best 0.78).

**Leakage / easy path**: windows within an episode are separated by `MIN_DELAY_H = 144` (line 78) so no target hour appears in another window's context; history block (first 3601 h) precedes all eval windows; no weather or cross-building data. Trivial baselines: previous-day (adapter reference, line 317) is the WEAKEST published baseline; the 7-day hour mean (avg7) is 0.82-0.83x of it in the paper and passes the 0.90 acceptance threshold (`ACCEPT_MARGIN`, line 77) on 3 of our 5 episodes.

**Verdict: CONSISTENT on ratios; absolute values NOT-COMPARABLE.** Agent beats prev-day by about 21 % (paper best 22 %) and avg7 by about 7 % (paper best 5 %), i.e. about fine-tuned-Transformer level using only per-building history; plausible, not SUSPICIOUSLY-HIGH. Slice too small to separate 0.79 from 0.85.

**To-do**
1. `for33_buildingsbench.py:317` (`persistence`) and `:77`: switch the reference to avg7 (the official `AveragePersistence` = hour-of-day mean of the last 7 days), otherwise acceptance passes for a trivial average.
2. `:80` (`M_TARGET`): use >= 7 target days per building or >= 20 buildings per role, otherwise the per-building median CVRMSE is dominated by one day.
3. `:296`: publish commercial and residential medians separately (6-36 vs 67-95); the balanced mean mixes scales.

**Comparability**: NOT comparable in absolute terms; consistent in range. Ours: 16 molecules with 4 actives per episode (48 active-inactive pairs), training on 4,000 molecules sampled from `train` (about 8 x fewer than 32.9 k), IID=valid, OOD=test scaffolds. Single-episode AUC has SD about 0.13-0.16 (simulation with 4/12 split), so a single episode cannot separate 0.75 from 0.85; even the pooled CI (+-0.12) covers the whole leaderboard from 0.66 to 0.91. **Parity**: `roc_auc_score` from sklearn (lines 230, 246, 374) = OGB `Evaluator('ogbg-molhiv')` (sklearn `roc_auc_score`, checked with the `ogb` package, no diff). No ties in scores (0 ties in all 5 episodes).

**Leakage / easy path** (`dup34.py`): no canonical-SMILES duplicates between the 4,000-molecule train sample and eval/dev molecules; only 2 Bemis-Murcko scaffolds shared between train and valid/test (as expected for a scaffold split); label file is OGB's. Trivial reference (L2 logistic regression on 11 count features) reaches AUC 0.56-0.79 per episode (the data team measured 0.690 on a larger pool). The Morgan-FP RF family is the natural comparison: our 0.80 pooled with 4k training molecules is in line with RF on 33k (0.81-0.82).

**Verdict: CONSISTENT (absolute NOT-COMPARABLE).** No sign of a leak or bug; the noise budget is the issue.
